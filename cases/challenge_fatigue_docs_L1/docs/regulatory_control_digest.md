# Public regulatory control digest

This synthetic digest guides an evaluation exercise. It is not legal or accounting advice and does not determine compliance.

The [PRA Supervisory Statement SS1/23](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/prudential-regulation/publication/2023/may/model-risk-management-principles-for-banks-ss) sets expectations across model governance, development, implementation, independent validation and risk mitigants. The Alder review uses those themes when checking ownership, version-specific approval and validation, implementation evidence and monitoring.

The [EBA Guidelines on PD estimation, LGD estimation and the treatment of defaulted exposures](https://www.eba.europa.eu/activities/single-rulebook/regulatory-activities/model-validation/guidelines-pd-estimation-lgd) provide public context for PD, LGD, default treatment, data quality and estimation choices. The challenge case uses general examples such as consistent default routes, recovery treatment and parameter implementation without claiming to assess IRB compliance.

[IFRS 9 Financial Instruments](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-9-financial-instruments/) provides the public basis for expected credit losses, significant increases in credit risk, lifetime measurement and forward-looking information. The standard includes a rebuttable presumption at more than 30 days past due and requires the assessment to consider changes in default risk since initial recognition. The supplied programme checks internal consistency and evidence rather than issuing an accounting conclusion.
