# Public regulatory control digest

This synthetic digest guides an evaluation exercise. It is not legal or accounting advice and does not determine compliance.

The [PRA Supervisory Statement SS1/23](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/prudential-regulation/publication/2023/may/model-risk-management-principles-for-banks-ss) describes expectations for model identification and risk classification, governance, development and implementation, independent validation, and risk mitigants. The Alder review uses those themes when assessing ownership, approval, validation evidence, limitations and monitoring.

The [EBA Guidelines on PD estimation, LGD estimation and the treatment of defaulted exposures](https://www.eba.europa.eu/activities/single-rulebook/regulatory-activities/model-validation/guidelines-pd-estimation-lgd) inform the synthetic checks concerning probability of default, loss given default, default treatment, data and estimation choices.

The [Basel Framework credit-risk principles](https://www.bis.org/basel_consolidated_guidelines/chapter/CRI/10.htm) provide public context for credit-risk parameter use and controls. The [IFRS 9 Financial Instruments standard page](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-9-financial-instruments/) provides public context for expected credit loss and impairment. The supplied programme checks internal consistency and evidence; it does not reproduce either publication or claim a full regulatory assessment.
